Beslut

Förvaltningschef beslutar att medfinansiera projektet med 25 000 kr.

Sökandes   sammanfattning

Havana Soul is a cultural project born from the idea of bringing a culture from Cuba rich in
vibration and color like Cuban popular culture. It is a project that combines two artistic
manifestations, (music and dance) creating a new show for the Swedish audience in general.
The show consists of a band of four Cuban musicians playing jazz, Latin jazz, bolero and
other different genres of Cuban music. Accompanied by audiovisual material and a dance
couple who tell a story to the public. Giving an authentic piece of Cuban culture to the
Swedish community. The show lasts 1h 15 min. of which 15min are breaks. The musicians
and dancers in the show are all professional Cuban artists living in Europe today. Havana
Soul is a project created with the intention of promoting quality and artistic diversity in
society. Arousing emotions and leaving a lasting impression on everyone. I am going to
Cuba in october/november and will make the video for the show in Cuba to get the right
atmosphere and surroundings to create the material audiovisual. Also the marketing video
will be produced there. This application is regarding this production and the start for the
rest of the show. For the rest of the show I will seek money from sources available that
have later decision dates than I can wait for regarding this video and my travel to Cuba.
