Sammanfattning

Briefly summarize what should be done, why it should be done, for whom it is
being done, and where implementation is planned to take place Andersabo is a
residency project for Scandinavian and international music and art which
is based in a farmhouse in Unnaryd, Region Halland. Since 2016, we have hosted

approximately 50 musicians and artists to have residencies at the house, resulting
in a range of new works, collaborations, tours, and dialogues between different
cultures, experienced in the local environment of Halland.
For 2020 and 2021, we are continuing to develop and grow the project, inviting
musicians from Denmark, Norway, Sweden, and the United States to come and

live and work at the house. Each residency will allow the invited artists to develop
new work, to collaborate, and to present their work to the audience in Halland. I
believe that the project should take place because it provides an opportunity for a
diverse range of Nordic and international

artists to develop their work, to respond to the unique context of Region Halland,
and to bring a range of art and music to the audience of Halland. The project's
focus is always to support both the artists in making their work, and in providing an
accessible and open experience for the local audience in Halland, allowing them to
have new experiences of art, music, and culture. The project will take place at the

Andersabo house, as well as at Unnaryd's church. We will be working with local
organizers, musicians, a local festival,
and a local farm.

Beslut utifrån ansökan om stöd för kulturutveckling (projekt)
Beslutar enligt 3A i Driftnämnden Kultur och skolas förteckning över delegerade

ärenden att medfinansiera ansökan från Jason Dungan för projektet
Andersabo Musik med 70000 kr för 2020 och 130000 kr för 2021.

Eventuell beslutad precisering för beviljade medel anges här: Enligt skriftlig
komplettering där önskan om att byta 2020 års innehåll mot 2021 och omvänt på
grund av rådande pandemiläge bifalles 2021 års summa 2020, 70 000 kr och 2020
års summa 2021, 130 000 kr.
