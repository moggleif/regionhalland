Sammanfattning

Laundry Service is a supernatural thriller about loneliness, loss and a sense of guilt. Set in
the Swedish countryside, this short film is focused on exploring narrative and visual
possibilities of the 16 mm film medium, which is a principal technical and expressive tool.
A contrast of exterior landscapes and claustrophobic interiors plays an important role in
revealing the main character's state of mind and her phobias. The themes we are eager to

explore within this story include inner conflict, psychological instability of a young woman
in response to a loss of her sibling, and a certain ambiguity of supernatural elements that
can be interpreted as the protagonist's hallucinations. In terms of genre, the project is
inspired by vintage horror movies of the 80s and 90s with their twisted and tragic endings,
where the borders between supernatural phenomenons and mental insanity are often
blurred.
