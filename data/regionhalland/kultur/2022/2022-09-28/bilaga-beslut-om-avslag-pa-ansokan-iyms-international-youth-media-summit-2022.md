Project title: IYMS goes Nordic
Case number: 28055
Decision: 2022-06-22 13:12:36

Dear applicant,

The Nordic Culture Fund has now processed your funding application. Based on an overall
assessment of the applications it has received, the fund has decided that the application can not
be prioritized.

The Fund’s assessment of your application is:

the project presents an interesting vision for developing a Nordic version of IYMS - a Nordic
Youth Media Summit, but the content of the proposed project is inadequately described: the
project plan does not adequately support the project’s ambitions and Nordic collaboration.

In this allocation round the Fund has received 133 valid applications, and of these, 37 have
received funding. An overview of current grants is available at www.nordiskkulturfond.org

The decisions of the fund are final and there is no appeals procedure.
