SBLEKINGE

Letter of Intent

Included parties in the Småland Blekinge Halland South Sweden's (SBHSS) Health Platform
network and Emilia-Romagna agree to undertake a process of collaboration. It is intended that
this collaboration will lead towards closer links between both parties for mutual learning and
improvement with the aim to participate in mutual EU projects.

Area of primary interest and intended actions, in which both parties can work together for
mutual learning, an exchange of experiences, and development within health and wellbeing.

The overall aim of the collaboration is to support increased shared learning between both
parties, and to collaborate and develop within the agreed areas and aim to find common
solutions to mutual challenges within health and wellbeing.

Primary areas of co-operation and learning:

e - Creating value with health data

e Development of integrated care

e Community Health Center training

e  Elderly Care

e Mental health

e Differences in health outcome between ethnic groups
e Team development

e Work satisfaction

Intended actions:

Workshops
Webinars

Visits

Potential EU-projects

For the Emilia-Romagna Region — Italy

Luca Baldino, General Director of Emilia-Romagna Health Care Authority
