How    can    Northern       Europe      become        a

green     Silicon     Valley?

OECD-  STRING seminar in Gothenburg November 17 2020

The OECD and STRING are conducting a Swedish national seminar on how Northern
Europe can become a green Silicon Valley- an internationally competitive green

megaregion, producing, developing, testing and championing green technologies and
sustainable infrastructure.

Participants will get the opportunity to provide their input and set the direction for the
coming strategic report that the cooperation between the OECD and STRING will result in.

The report will contain concrete political recommendations for the entire STRING area,
stretching from Hamburg to Oslo.

  Mayor of the City of Gothenburg, Axel Josefson and Member of the Regional
Executive Board for Region Västra Götaland, Kristina Jonäng, will open the
OECD seminar.

  Former European Commissioner for Climate Action, Connie Hedegaard, will
moderate the event.

  Head of the Urban Policies Review in the OECD Soo-Jin Kim will present the
direction of the concrete policy recommendations for the ScanMed corridor Oslo-
Gothenburg-Malmö-Copenhagen-Hamburg.

  President and CEO of AB Volvo, Marin Lundstedt, will talk about the importance
of infrastructure connectivity for Swedish competitiveness.

  Other key actors will provide their perspectives

Time and place

The seminar will take place at Centralen Conference, November 17th, from 09:30-13:30,
